Mission 2 — Prompt power

Vague vs specific prompts

Better prompts get better answers. Real tip from ChatGPT and Claude: be clear about what you want, who it’s for, and how you want it (format, length).

You need help understanding photosynthesis. Which prompt will get you a more useful answer?

Tell me about photosynthesis
Explain photosynthesis as if I'm 12, then give one real example and one simple analogy. Keep it under 200 words.

Which one is stronger and why?

Real ChatGPT prompt: “Explain this concept to me as if I am 12 years old, then explain it again in normal academic detail, then give me one real example and one analogy: [topic].”
Mission 3 — Real tools

Claude: Learning Mode & Artifacts

Claude has a Learning Mode that doesn’t just give you the answer — it asks you questions so you figure it out (Socratic method). Great for actually understanding.

Claude (Education) — Students use it for: study projects, career projects, and research. It can turn your handwritten notes into clean documents and create quizzes with its Artifacts feature.

ChatGPT: Study & homework help

ChatGPT is used for 24/7 study help, step-by-step explanations, and practice questions. The key is asking in a clear, structured way.

ChatGPT (students) — Example: “Help me solve: 3x + 7 = 22, showing each step.” Or: “Generate 10 exam-style questions from these notes. After I answer each, grade me and explain the correct answer.” Another: “Help me create a study schedule. I have 5 classes, 2 hours of homework each day, and soccer on Tuesdays. I want to improve my grades but keep balance.”
