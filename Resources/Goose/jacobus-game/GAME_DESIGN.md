# JACOBUS: WILLIAMSBURG WARRIOR
## Game Design Document

### 🎮 Concept
A manga-influenced, 80s-style beat 'em up where Jacobus, the legendary protector of Williamsburg and New York City, battles waves of street thugs, corrupt corporate agents, and supernatural threats to defend his beloved borough.

### 🎨 Visual Style
- **80s Aesthetic**: Neon colors, synthwave palette (hot pink, electric blue, purple, cyan)
- **Manga Influence**: Dynamic action poses, speed lines, dramatic facial expressions
- **Pixel Art**: 16-bit style graphics reminiscent of arcade classics
- **Urban Setting**: Brooklyn streets, graffiti-covered walls, iconic NYC landmarks

### 📖 Story
**The Year**: 198X  
**The Place**: Williamsburg, Brooklyn & New York City

Jacobus, a martial arts master and community guardian, must defend Williamsburg from the evil MAXCORP syndicate trying to gentrify and destroy the neighborhood's soul. With his signature bandana and street-fighting skills, Jacobus takes to the neon-lit streets to restore peace.

### 🕹️ Gameplay
- **Genre**: Side-scrolling beat 'em up / brawler
- **Perspective**: 2.5D side view with depth
- **Combat**: Combo-based fighting with special moves
- **Progression**: Multi-stage adventure through NYC locations

### 🎯 Core Mechanics
1. **Movement**: Walk left/right, jump, crouch
2. **Combat**: 
   - Light Attack (fast combo)
   - Heavy Attack (powerful strikes)
   - Special Moves (energy-based)
   - Grab & Throw
3. **Health System**: HP bar with pickup items
4. **Score System**: Points for defeating enemies and combos

### 🗺️ Stages
1. **Williamsburg Streets** - Bedford Avenue at night
2. **Brooklyn Bridge** - Fighting on the iconic bridge
3. **Times Square** - Neon chaos in Manhattan
4. **MAXCORP Tower** - Final confrontation

### 👊 Characters

#### Jacobus (Player)
- Age: 28
- Appearance: Muscular build, signature red bandana, ripped jean jacket
- Fighting Style: Street brawler mixed with martial arts
- Special Move: "BROOKLYN FURY" - Devastating spinning kick

#### Enemies
- **Street Punks**: Basic thugs in leather jackets
- **MAXCORP Guards**: Corporate security in suits
- **Cyber Ninjas**: High-tech assassins
- **Boss - Mr. Suit**: MAXCORP CEO with powered exoskeleton

### 🎵 Audio
- Synthwave/Chiptune soundtrack
- Punch/kick sound effects
- Voice samples: "BROOKLYN POWER!" "Not in my neighborhood!"
- Retro game over/victory jingles

### 🎮 Controls
- **Arrow Keys/WASD**: Move
- **Z/J**: Light Attack
- **X/K**: Heavy Attack
- **C/L**: Special Move
- **SPACE**: Jump
- **ESC**: Pause

### 📊 Game Flow
```
Title Screen → Stage Select → Gameplay → Game Over/Victory → High Score → Loop
```

### 🎨 Color Palette
```
Primary: #FF006E (Hot Pink)
Secondary: #00F5FF (Electric Cyan)
Accent: #8B00FF (Purple)
Background: #0A0E27 (Dark Navy)
UI: #FFD700 (Gold/Yellow)
```

### 💾 Technical Stack
- **Engine**: Pygame (Python)
- **Resolution**: 800x600 (4:3 ratio - authentic 80s feel)
- **Frame Rate**: 60 FPS
- **Art**: Procedurally generated pixel art + custom sprites
